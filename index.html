Your followers tap through. Then they have to guess.

I checked 8 coach program pages on a phone. On 6 of them, the first screen didn't show who the program is for and one clear way to join. What I saw instead:

  • The program's name where "who it's for" should be
  • Several different buttons competing for one decision
  • The way to join buried a few screens down

If you've ever thought "I'm posting content but not getting clients," this can be part of it. A follower who can't tell if it's for them, or can't find how to join, may leave, or end up in your DMs asking.

Answer the two questions every visitor has.

Is this for me? How do I join? Your fixed first screen answers both, before your program's name or anything else.
